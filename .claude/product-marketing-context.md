# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-02-27*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Free online invoice generator — create professional invoices in seconds, no signup required.
**What it does:** Magic Invoice is a browser-based invoice editor that lets freelancers, small businesses, and anyone create beautiful, professional invoices in real-time with instant PDF export. All data stays in the browser — nothing is stored on servers. Supports 100+ currencies, 6 languages, digital signatures, and multiple export formats (PDF, JSON, CSV, XML).
**Product category:** Invoice generator, free invoice maker, online invoice creator, billing software
**Product type:** Free web app (open source, Apache 2.0)
**Business model:** Free tool — monetized through brand awareness, GitHub stars, community contributions, and Buy Me A Coffee donations. Potential for future premium features.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Solo freelancers, micro-businesses (1-10 employees), small businesses, independent contractors, service providers, consultants, agencies
**Decision-makers:** Business owners, freelancers, accountants, finance managers at small companies
**Primary use case:** Creating professional invoices quickly without signing up for expensive invoicing software
**Jobs to be done:**
- Create a professional-looking invoice in under 2 minutes
- Export invoices as PDF to send to clients
- Track payment status across multiple invoices
- Maintain consistent branding across invoices (logo, colors)
**Use cases:**
- Freelancer billing a client for a completed project
- Small business sending monthly invoices to recurring customers
- Consultant creating a one-off invoice for advisory work
- Service provider generating invoices in multiple currencies for international clients
- Anyone who needs a quick invoice without installing software or creating an account

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Creating professional invoices is either expensive (paid software with monthly subscriptions) or painful (ugly Word/Excel templates that look unprofessional).
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Paid invoicing software (FreshBooks, QuickBooks, Zoho Invoice) requires signup, credit card, and monthly fees ($15-50/mo) — overkill for occasional invoicing
- Free invoice templates (Word, Google Docs, Excel) look unprofessional and require manual calculation
- Other free invoice generators require account creation, store your data on their servers, or have limited features
- Many tools are bloated with features small users don't need (accounting, time tracking, project management)
**What it costs them:** Time wasted formatting invoices manually, unprofessional appearance hurting credibility, paying for software they barely use, privacy concerns with cloud-stored financial data
**Emotional tension:** Frustration with complex software, anxiety about data privacy, embarrassment sending ugly invoices, resentment paying monthly fees for occasional use

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Invoice Ninja, Wave, Zoho Invoice Free — fall short because they require signup/account creation and store your data on their servers
**Secondary:** Word/Excel/Google Docs invoice templates — fall short because they look unprofessional, require manual calculations, and lack PDF export quality
**Indirect:** Full accounting suites (QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks) — fall short because they're expensive overkill for simple invoicing needs

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Zero signup required — start creating immediately
- 100% browser-based — no data leaves your device (true privacy)
- Open source (Apache 2.0) — transparent, trustworthy, community-driven
- Real-time preview — see exactly what your invoice looks like as you type
- Multi-language support (English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Greek)
- 100+ currencies supported
- Digital signature support
- Multiple export formats (PDF, JSON, CSV, XML)
**How we do it differently:** Everything runs in the browser. No server, no database, no account. Your financial data never leaves your device.
**Why that's better:** Complete privacy, instant access, zero friction, no vendor lock-in
**Why customers choose us:** Speed (invoice in under 2 minutes), privacy (no data stored), cost (completely free), simplicity (no learning curve)
